A modern, feature-rich expense tracking application built with PHP and MySQL, designed to help Kenyans manage their personal finances effectively.

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Dashboard & Analytics
- **Real-time Statistics**: Track daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly expenses
- **Visual Charts**: Interactive pie charts and expense visualizations
- **Quick Overview**: Instant view of your financial health

### 💳 Expense Management
- **Add Expenses**: Quick and easy expense entry with categories
- **Expense History**: Complete record of all your spending
- **Search & Filter**: Find expenses by date, category, or amount
- **Notes & Details**: Add descriptions and notes to each expense

### 📈 Advanced Reporting
- **Date-wise Reports**: Filter expenses by specific date ranges
- **Monthly Summaries**: View spending patterns by month
- **Yearly Overview**: Annual expense analysis and trends
- **Export Ready**: Reports formatted for easy sharing

### 🔐 User Management
- **Secure Registration**: Easy account creation with validation
- **Password Recovery**: Forgot password functionality with secure tokens
- **User Profiles**: Personal information management
- **Session Security**: Protected user sessions
